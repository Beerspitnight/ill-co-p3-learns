~Who is Lloyd?~

Why am I here? What is going on?

Built to help middle-aged guys escape mediocrity and fire up new brain pathways, this project started off as a Number Guessing Game, then it turned into chaos. Chaos is cool, sometimes. Tag stuff. Learn stuff. Maybe crash a server.

You want me to do what?

Look at images. Click a dropdown or two. Maybe hit a radio button. That’s it. That’s what we need from you. We don't know anything about design, so we need you to share your knowledge so we can teach our AI incarnation, who we call AssAI, how to design cool stuff.

So What *Is* This?

The ill-Co-P3 project is your stoney 🌲🌬️ design bootcamp in disguise. Tag images, whisper critiques to baby AIs, and vibe with principles like Balance, Unity, and “Who designed this font?”

You want me to do this for free??

The ill-Co-P3 should probably pay you, but they are simply groupings of excited electrons, so good luck collecting.

What is the deal with the name?

"Who knows where throughts come from? They just appear."

Lloyd Dobler: the trench-coated protagonist of the seminal 1989 film *Say Anything* (arguably the finest year of the '80s, possibly the '90s too — hell, I wish I was still in ’89). Before there was The Dude, there was Lloyd. Lloyd — an amalgam of every archetype in your high school cafeteria — stood as the existential counterweight to the Reagan-era transactionality that defined so much of '80s pop culture. He wasn’t trying to sell anything, buy anything, or process anything. He just wanted to stand in the rain, hold up a boombox, and believe in something — even if that something was a Peter Gabriel song. More than just a hopeless romantic, Lloyd represented the distilled opposite of Gordon Gekko. Two years after *Wall Street* made “Greed is good” gospel, *Say Anything* whispered, “Yeah… but so is awkward sincerity and a killer mixtape.”
-Beerspitnight
